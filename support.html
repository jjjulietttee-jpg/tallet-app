Common snags

I paid but Pro is still locked.

Open Business, then tap Restore purchases. Check that this iPhone uses the same Apple ID as the purchase. If it stays locked, mail us the date of purchase.

My phone was replaced. Where are my estimates?

Tallet keeps documents on the device, so they come across only through an iPhone backup or a phone-to-phone transfer. We have no server copy. Until you finish the transfer, send any open invoices as PDFs from the old phone.

How do I remove a document?

In the document list, use Delete on that estimate or invoice. It cannot be brought back afterwards.

Can I reuse an estimate as an invoice?

Yes. Open an approved estimate and use the invoice action; the line items carry over, and the numbering follows the prefixes in your Business settings.

Why does the app show only one contact?

That is deliberate: Tallet uses the iOS chooser and reads just the card you pick, so it never browses your contacts.

My logo looks wrong or did not load.

Choose a square or wide image in the Business tab. Very large photos are shrunk on save. If the picker reports an error, try another image.

How do I stop the trial?

iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Tallet, cancel. Do it at least a day before the trial ends.

Where do the PDFs go?

They are built on the phone and only leave through the share sheet you open.

How do I wipe everything?

Delete the documents you no longer want one by one, or remove the app from the iPhone, which clears all of its stored data.
