Common questions

Where are my documents stored? Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud copy.

How do I restore my subscription? Open Business → Restore purchases, or tap Restore on the Tallet Pro screen.

How do I cancel? Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Tallet.

Can clients pay through Tallet? No. Tallet records the payments you receive in cash, check, card or transfer and keeps the balance.
