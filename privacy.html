Privacy, in contractor language

Revised October 6, 2026

Your quotes, your customers and your payment notes are kept inside the app on your own iPhone. We run no database of them and have no login to hand out. The only outside service is the one that tells us whether you have paid for Pro.

Picking a customer from Contacts

The app opens the standard iOS contact chooser. Only the card you tap is passed to Tallet, to fill name, phone, email and address. The rest of your address book remains invisible to it.

Picking a logo

The same idea applies to photos: the system picker hands over the single image you select, and no library access is requested.

Sending a document

An estimate or invoice becomes a PDF made on the phone. It travels only if you press share and choose Mail, Messages, AirDrop or another destination. From that point the receiving service’s own rules apply, and we cannot see or recall it.

Money handling

Tallet is a bookkeeping aid, not a payment processor. It has no card form, no bank link and no way to pull funds. Whatever you note as paid is simply a number you typed.

The one outside service

Subscriptions are sold by Apple. For checking whether Pro is active, Tallet uses Adapty, an analytics-and-paywall provider. Adapty gets an anonymous profile identifier generated for the install, the purchase receipt data, and the paywall that was displayed. It does not get your customers, documents, contacts or photos. Apple keeps your payment method; we never see it.

Reminder notification

If you begin a free trial, the app can ask permission to post a single local notice the day before the trial ends. It is scheduled on the phone itself, with no push server. Decline it, or switch it off in iOS Settings, and nothing else changes.

Email you send us

The Write to us option opens your mail app with a prefilled draft naming the build number, the iOS release you run and your Adapty profile ID, which helps us trace a bug. You can delete those lines before sending.

Tracking

No advertising ID is read, no third-party ad network is built in, and nothing follows you between apps.

Getting rid of data

Open the document list and use Delete on an estimate or invoice to remove it. Deleting the Tallet app from the iPhone wipes everything it stored, including the database and logo. Backups made by iCloud or a computer follow your own device settings. Because we hold nothing on a server, there is no copy for us to erase on request.

Young users

The app is meant for working adults, and we do not knowingly serve anyone under 13.
