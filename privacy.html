Privacy Policy

Tallet: Estimate & Invoice · Last updated September 25, 2026

Tallet makes estimates and invoices for contractors. It is built so that your business records stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no server that stores your documents, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

Your business profile and logo, clients and their contact details, estimates, invoices, line items, payments you record and your price book. Tallet keeps them in its own storage on your iPhone and never uploads them.

Contacts and Photos

When you pick a client from Contacts, iOS shows its own picker and Tallet receives only the one contact you chose. When you add a logo, iOS shows the photo picker and Tallet receives only the image you chose. Tallet does not read your address book or photo library.

Sharing PDFs

A PDF leaves your iPhone only when you share it yourself (Mail, Messages, AirDrop and so on). What happens to it after that is governed by the app or service you send it with.

Payments

Tallet only records payments you mark as received. It does not process card payments and never sees card or bank details.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Tallet receives only the status of your subscription, never your payment details. We may use a subscription-management service (Adapty) that receives an anonymous app-install identifier and purchase status to validate purchases; it does not receive your documents or clients.

Analytics and tracking

Tallet does not track you across apps or websites and does not use advertising identifiers.

Deleting your data

Delete documents, clients or rates in the app, or delete Tallet from your iPhone to remove all of its data.

Children

Tallet is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
